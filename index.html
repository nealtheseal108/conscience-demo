Conscience

Neal's account Wednesday, Nov 4.

Conscience does the work on your to-do list, then stops at the one step only you can take.

For students juggling classes, clubs and life. It finds tasks in your connected accounts and prepares the work from your files, so it's ready when you look.

How I decided

I was juggling classes, three research labs and a job search. Every app I tried only reminded me about things. I wanted one that does the work and stops at the one step only I can take.

What I used

A Node.js app built on Claude, with a local Llama 3.2 model that reads my texts on my Mac. It connects to Outlook, Gmail, my calendar and iMessage.

The impact

It holds every open task I have and has the drafts, plans and research ready before I open them. It tracks my fall job search: 160 applications and 10 interviews.
